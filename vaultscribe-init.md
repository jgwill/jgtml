## FRACTAL_TRADING_ADVENTURE_GUIDE.md (Markdown)
A whimsical, story-driven guide to fractal trading inspired by Bill Williams. Introduces the adventure of spotting fractal patterns, befriending the Alligators (market indicators), and mastering the five dimensions of market awareness. Designed to teach traders to see hidden order in market chaos.

## MAGICAL_INDICATORS_GUIDE.md (Markdown)
A bestiary of magical market indicators: Alligators, Fractal Butterflies, Oscillators, and Gann tools. Explains how each indicator works, their mystical properties, and how they combine to reveal market truths. Emphasizes the synergy of using multiple indicators for deeper insight.

## MAGICAL_SPELLS_EXPLAINED.md (Markdown)
A spellbook of trading strategies, each described as a magical spell. Details seven core spells and advanced combinations, blending indicators and market logic into actionable rituals. Includes wisdom on risk management and journaling for continuous improvement.
